\section{Graphs and their nulls}\label{sec:graphs}

\paragraph{Relations.}
Four point-in-time relations are edge tables with validity intervals; an edge is used on date $d$ iff $\mathrm{valid\_from}\le d<\mathrm{valid\_to}$ (Table~\ref{tab:relations}). Only features and hidden states pass along edges; no neighbour's target is ever aggregated.

\paragraph{Rewired-graph null.}
Whether the topology carries information beyond degree and group membership is addressed by training the same network on graphs that preserve those quantities and destroy the pairing \citep{maslovsneppen2002}, although the paired test against it was not completed (Section~\ref{sec:res_graphs}). One rewired table per relation is drawn per seed and held fixed through time. For E1, SIC4 timelines are permuted among firms with the identical SIC2 timeline (SIC2 kept exactly, SIC4 group sizes approximately, and about 12\% of firms keep their label). For E4, degree-preserving double-edge swaps act on the unique (customer, supplier) pairs within customer-SIC4 strata (fallback SIC3, SIC2), with realised swap shares of 0.30 to 0.39 per snapshot. For E5, the use table is permuted within columns per data year, so each buying industry keeps its supplier count. For CORR, each firm and year gets a fixed random ranking of the other firms by stratum (SIC2 $\times$ beta tercile, then SIC2, division, any), and on each date the in-neighbours are the best-ranked present firms, as many as the real in-degree, with the real sorted weights reassigned.

\paragraph{Label-shift diagnostic.}
A model that has memorised firm identity, or a slowly varying attribute leaking through the features, predicts last year's label about as well as this year's. For each refit every training row (firm $f$, formation $i$) is relabelled with $f$'s label at formation $i-K$ (else $i-K-1$, else $i-K+1$), $K=54/13/5$ for W/M/Q, valid only if that source window ended at least 366 days before the row's formation; rows without a valid source are dropped. The declared read rule is that the signal ``persists under shift'' if the shifted development IC is positive, has $t\ge2$ and is at least half the real IC, and a pass that persists would be relabelled ``not attributable to current information''.
