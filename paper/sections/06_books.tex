\section{From predictions to a book}\label{sec:books}

\paragraph{Decile book.}
Bin each date by the percentile rank of the prediction, hold the top decile long and the bottom decile short with equal weights within a leg (one dollar each side), and pay cost $c$ per side on the two-sided turnover $\tau_t=\|w^{new}-w^{prev}\|_1$ against the previous target weights:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:decile}
g_t=\bar y_{long}-\bar y_{short},\qquad \mathrm{net}_t=g_t-c\,\tau_t .
\end{equation}
This is the book of Section~\ref{sec:power} and Table~\ref{tab:crisis}; the earlier window-grid study's decile book drifted the previous weights by the holding return, $w^{drift}_i=w_i(1+y_i)/(1+\bar y_{leg})$, before differencing.

\paragraph{The grid construction.}
The grid construction controls the book's volatility, industry and market-beta exposure. Let $m_t$ be the tradable set (present, finite score, universe filter, at least 50 names). The \emph{aim} $A_{i,t}$ is the centred rank of the prediction over all predicted names, $(R_i-\frac12)/n-\frac12$, or for the multi-horizon blends the mean of the W, M and Q (or W and M) centred ranks, slower horizons carried forward from their latest formation. The aim is re-ranked over $m_t$ to $c_i$; with $\sigma_i$ the 63-session realised volatility (missing to the median, floored at a fixed 5th-percentile constant), four maps give the weights:
\begin{align}
u_i&=c_i/\sigma_i,\qquad \mathcal N(v)_i=\frac{2\,(v_i-\bar v)}{\sum_{k:\,v_k\ne0}|v_k-\bar v|}\ \text{for }v_i\ne0,\quad \mathcal N(v)_i=0\text{ for }v_i=0,\label{eq:norm}\\
w^{(1)}&=\mathcal N(u),\qquad w^{(2)}_i=w^{(1)}_i-\overline{w^{(1)}}_{g(i)},\qquad w^{(3)}=\mathcal N(w^{(2)}),\label{eq:demean}\\
a&=\mathcal N\big(w^{(3)}-X(X^\top X)^{-1}X^\top w^{(3)}\big),\qquad X=[\mathbf 1,\ \beta],\label{eq:neutral}
\end{align}
$\bar v$ the mean over non-zero entries. The group $g(i)$ is the SIC4 group if it has at least three names, else the SIC2 group among the remaining names if it has at least three, else the SIC division. Equation~\eqref{eq:neutral} projects onto the complement of $\mathrm{span}\{\mathbf 1,\beta\}$, $\beta$ the 252-session beta to the equal-weight universe, so $\sum_ia_i=0$, $\sum_i|a_i|=2$ and $a^\top\beta=0$ hold exactly; the industry demeaning holds only approximately after the projection, and for $\theta<1$ the held book mixes targets built on earlier betas, so its beta exposure is small but not zero.

\paragraph{Partial adjustment, cost and statistics.}
With $\theta\in\{0.05,0.1,0.2,0.5,1\}$ and $\mathbb 1_{m_t}$ the tradable mask,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:partial}
w_t=\mathbb 1_{m_t}\odot\big(w_{t-1}+\theta(a_t-w_{t-1})\big),\qquad g_t=w_t^\top y^{raw}_t,\qquad \tau_t=\|w_t-w_{t-1}\|_1,\qquad \mathrm{net}_t=g_t-c\,\tau_t .
\end{equation}
Partial adjustment trades a mean information lag of $(1-\theta)/\theta$ periods against turnover, and because $w_0=0$ and masking only removes weight, $\sum_i|w_i|\le2$ at all times. Turnover is charged against undrifted previous weights; drift-charging changes every computed graph-cell Sharpe ratio by at most 0.01. With $P=52$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:sr}
\widehat{\SR}=\frac{\bar x}{s_x}\sqrt{P},\qquad \mathrm{ann}=\bar x\,P,\qquad \MaxDD=\min_k\Big(\frac{C_k}{\max_{j\le k}C_j}-1\Big),\quad C_k=\prod_{s\le k}(1+x_s).
\end{equation}

\paragraph{Grid, selection and pass rules.}
The grid has 55 cells, crossing the aims R1 (weekly), R2 (quarterly, carried forward and traded weekly), R4 (W+M+Q) and R4b (W+M) with the universes ALL, LIQ and TOP500 (R2 has no TOP500) and five values of $\theta$. The declared cost per side is 15 bps for ALL, 5 bps for LIQ and 2 bps for TOP500, and every cell is also stored at 1, 2, 5 and 15 bps. \emph{Selection}: among cells with development $\MaxDD\ge-25\%$ at the declared cost, the highest development net Sharpe ratio. \emph{Pass}: net $\SR\ge1.5$ on development \emph{and} on the check window with $\MaxDD\ge-25\%$ on both. A graph cell additionally needs, on every horizon its aim uses, a development paired $\Delta\IC>0$ (demeaned basis, Section~\ref{sec:infer}) with month-level $t\ge2$ against both C0 and C4, and would be relayed only after an independent adversarial review with three pre-agreed reproductions (retraining without price-level features, drift-charged turnover, block-bootstrap standard errors). Both rules were written before the corresponding books were read: for the graph programme before any graph book, and for the 55-cell grid before its read on the rebuilt panels (the same grid had been read on development only on an older build).
