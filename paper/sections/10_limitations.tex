\section{Limitations}\label{sec:limits}

\paragraph{Survivorship.}
The price feed lists firms as of October 2026; failures, acquisitions and firms that went dark are absent, which biases a long-short spread in a direction that is not fixed \citep{shumway1997}. For the monthly decile legs of the booster (2014 to 2023), on SEC filing dates and 8-K items with fixed return-at-death assumptions (failure $-40\%$, acquisition $+20\%$, dark $-20\%$) and blank-check companies excluded, the central estimate of the final-year channel's understatement of the spread is 0.2 to 0.45 percentage points per year, at most about 2.2 across variants, and its sign is not identified (it flips if the short leg is acquisition-heavy within its bins); the weekly books were not measured, and the level channel (about two thirds of 2013 filers had stopped filing by 2026) is not bounded. The models load on the characteristics most flattered by survivorship (short-term reversal, idiosyncratic volatility); an ablation without them improves the ensemble out of sample, which we report as a diagnostic only. A delisting-inclusive panel is the one change that would settle this.

\paragraph{Reused check window.}
The 2019 to 2022 window had been read three times before the graph experiments; the graph programme is its fourth use. The 2023 read is the only genuinely held-out year and is eleven months long; the frozen rule has 8 to 12\% power at the development IC of 0.024 (14 to 17\% at 0.03), so its pass is weak evidence as an event, and the observed IC of $\numICTwentyThree$ is the stronger evidence.

\paragraph{Price-level measurement error.}
The feed's closes are split-adjusted at download date and the panel builder undoes only the splits inside the loaded window, so the \$5 filter and the five price-level characteristics (\texttt{log\_price}, \texttt{log\_mcap}, \texttt{bm}, \texttt{ep}, \texttt{sp}) are wrong by the product of later splits for about 4 to 7\% of firm-months. This is a measurement error tied to future corporate actions rather than look-ahead on returns, and a variant without the filter and the five characteristics replicates the out-of-window IC ($+0.050$ against $+0.057$).

\paragraph{Incomplete graph programme.}
Section~\ref{sec:res_graphs} lists the runs not completed before the pause. The conclusion rests on C0, C0g, C1, C2 and C3 with ICs, books and paired $\Delta\IC$ against C0 and C0g on all horizons, and on C4 and C5 monthly and quarterly ICs; for C1 to C3 the missing comparisons cannot produce a pass, and C5 remains open.

\paragraph{Library defaults.}
The booster's $\lambda$ and $\gamma$ and the pairwise pair sampling are xgboost 2.1.4 defaults, and the deep models ran under torch 2.14.1. We state these defaults and versions but did not control them.
