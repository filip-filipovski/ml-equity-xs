\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

On a free, point-in-time, survivor-only US equity panel, a pre-fixed shallow booster carries a rank signal that replicates out of its development window and on one held-out year, but that signal is too small to become a net Sharpe ratio of 1.5 under any declared construction or cost. The shortfall lies in the information, of which the least demanding cell would need, to first order, at least about $\numKappaLeastBoth\times$ as much. On the development window the three completed graph networks, trained end to end, add no detectable information over the same features without a graph by paired $\Delta\IC$, and the rewired-graph comparison, completed only as monthly and quarterly IC levels, points the same way, while the temporal network C5 has no paired comparison and remains open. What the study leaves standing is its method, namely a validation geometry in which no two labels on opposite sides of a fit, validation or test boundary share a daily return, a cost-ladder identity that separates information from cost, a rewired-graph null, and a power calculation that puts confirmation of a Sharpe-0.3 property decades away. A delisting-inclusive panel and a forward prediction log would change what can be said next.
