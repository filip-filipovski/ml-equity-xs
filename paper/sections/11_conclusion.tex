\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

On a free, point-in-time, survivor-only US equity panel, a pre-fixed shallow booster carries a cross-sectional rank signal that replicates out of its development window and on one held-out year, and that signal is too small to become a Sharpe ratio of 1.5 by any portfolio construction or cost assumption we declared: the shortfall is information, by a factor of about $\numKappaLeastBoth$ in the least demanding cell. Graph neural networks trained end to end on economic and statistical firm graphs add no information to the same features without a graph, as measured by paired $\Delta\IC$ against an identical network; the rewired-graph comparison, completed only as IC levels on the monthly and quarterly horizons, points the same way. The methodological content that stands is the geometry that keeps labels from sharing returns, the cost-ladder identity that separates information from cost, the rewired-graph null, and a power calculation that places confirmation of a Sharpe-0.3 property decades away. A delisting-inclusive panel and a forward prediction log are the two additions that would change what can be said next.
