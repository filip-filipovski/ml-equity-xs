\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

On a free, point-in-time, survivor-only US equity panel, a pre-fixed shallow booster carries a rank signal that replicates out of its development window and on one held-out year, and that signal is too small to become a net Sharpe ratio of 1.5 under any declared construction or cost: the shortfall is information, about $\numKappaLeastBoth\times$ in the least demanding cell. Graph networks trained end to end add no information over the same features without a graph by paired $\Delta\IC$; the rewired-graph comparison, completed only as monthly and quarterly IC levels, points the same way. What stands is the method: a validation geometry in which no two labels on opposite sides of a fit, validation or test boundary share a daily return, a cost-ladder identity that separates information from cost, a rewired-graph null, and a power calculation that puts confirmation of a Sharpe-0.3 property decades away. A delisting-inclusive panel and a forward prediction log would change what can be said next.
