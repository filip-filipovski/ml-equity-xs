\section{Reproducibility}\label{app:repro}

Every table and figure is generated by two scripts (\path{paper/build_tables.py}, \path{build_figures.py}) from stored result files; no table number is typed by hand, and the numbers quoted in the text are macros that the same script writes. Sources under \path{results/}: the 55-cell grid and its cost ladder (\path{p12c_eval.json}), the cost-ladder inversion (\path{p12c_what_it_takes.json}), Sharpe standard errors (\path{p12c_precision.json}), the expected maximum of $N$ and the zero-cost benchmarks (\path{p12_stage4.json}), the out-of-window and hold-out IC reads (\path{p9_e6.json}, \path{p9_b_wfa.json}, \path{p9_b_fullhistory.json}), the monthly series (\path{p9_b_followups.json}), the power calculation (\path{p9_b_forward_power.json}); and the graph programme's generated results file (\path{results/gnn/RESULTS.md}, with \path{STATE.json}). A self-test reproduces the stored cost ladder from eq.~\eqref{eq:ladder} for all 55 cells and the stored expected maximum of 294 null Sharpe ratios by simulation.

The graph programme's hyperparameters are one set, hashed (\path{872821b168bcbcc3}) and registered before any graph result was read. Prediction files carry the panel fingerprint, hyperparameter hash and code hash and must reproduce the exact row sets of the grid (1{,}347{,}743 / 318{,}475 / 103{,}945 rows), and $\numPthirteenRefits$ refits were trained. The pre-registration documents of both programmes are in \path{docs/}.

\paragraph{Zone attestation.} Only training-zone rows were used (formation months 2009-01 to 2022-12, maximum formation date 2022-12-20 weekly). The 2023 walk-forward rows were scored once under a frozen rule (an earlier script loaded and masked them without scoring), and no price or return from the sealed zone from 2024-07-01 has been read (the survivorship diagnostic's 2026-vintage filing-status table reaches into it for two 2023 formations, with no returns). The check window 2019 to 2022 was used as a check four times, counted in Section~\ref{sec:valid}.
