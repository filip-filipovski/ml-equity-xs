\section{Data and the point-in-time panel}\label{sec:data}

\paragraph{Universe and formation grid.}
Formation dates $t$ are weekly (W, every fifth session from 2004-01-02), monthly (M, last session) or quarterly (Q, last session of each quarter). ``ALL'' is every US common stock with a bar on $t$, raw close $\ge\$5$ and 63-session median dollar volume $\ge\$1$M; ``LIQ'' is the half of ALL above the within-date median of that volume; ``TOP500'' the 500 largest by it. Prices are survivor-only: the feed lists firms as of October 2026 (Section~\ref{sec:limits}). $r_{i,s}$ is the daily adjusted-close return (missing if above 3 or below $-0.95$) and $M_s$ the equal-weight mean return of ALL fixed at the latest month-end before $s$. The panels run to 2022-12 with 1{,}664{,}463 weekly, 397{,}417 monthly and 132{,}961 quarterly firm-dates, of which 1{,}347{,}743 / 318{,}475 / 103{,}945 receive out-of-sample predictions.

\paragraph{Characteristics.}
Table~\ref{tab:features} lists the 28 characteristics at formation row $t$. Fundamentals use SEC facts accepted at or before the close of $t$, go stale after 18 months, and are set missing before 2009-01-01 and on rows with implied daily turnover above 10\%; trailing twelve months (TTM) is the sum of four quarters or the latest annual. Earnings events use 8-K item 2.02 filings with a 16:00~ET session rule.

\paragraph{Rank transform.}
Per date and characteristic, finite values are winsorised at the 1st and 99th percentiles, ranked with ties averaged ($R_i\in\{1,\dots,n\}$) and mapped to
\begin{equation}\label{eq:rank}
\tilde x_{i,f,t}=\frac{R_i-1}{n-1}-\frac12\in[-\tfrac12,\tfrac12],\qquad m_{i,f,t}=0;\qquad\text{missing: }\tilde x=0,\ m=1 .
\end{equation}
The input is $x_{i,t}=(\tilde x_{i,1..28},\ \text{11 SIC one-hots},\ m_{i,1..28})\in\R^{67}$; the one-hots (ten divisions plus ``unknown'') come from the current filer profile and are static. A 29th candidate (gross profitability) was dropped by a 30\% coverage rule fixed before any result was read.

\paragraph{Target.}
With $u$ the next formation row, entry at the close of row $t{+}1$ and exit at the close of row $u{+}1$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:yraw}
y^{raw}_{i,t}=\prod_{s=t+2}^{u+1}(1+r_{i,s})-1,\qquad
y^{rank}_{i,t}=\frac{R(y^{raw}_{i,t})-1}{n_t-1}-\frac12,\qquad
y^{ex}_{i,t}=y^{raw}_{i,t}-\overline{y^{raw}_{t}},
\end{equation}
ranks over ALL with ties averaged. Models fit $y^{rank}$, ICs are scored against $y^{ex}$, books are paid in $y^{raw}$. A within-date rank is not market-neutral: with $r_{i,t}=a_t+(\beta_i-\bar\beta)\,\mathrm{mkt}_t+\varepsilon_{i,t}$ it keeps the term $(\beta_i-\bar\beta)\,\mathrm{mkt}_t$, and the monthly rank target has correlation 0.75 with the market excess return; Section~\ref{sec:books} removes this at the portfolio stage.

\paragraph{Zones.}
The data are partitioned once: a training zone (2004-01 to 2023-01), a walk-forward zone (2023-01 to 2024-07) and a sealed zone from 2024-07-01 from which no price or return has been read. Inside the training zone, 2009--2018 is the development window on which every selection was made (some later design choices were proposed after earlier check-window results had been reported, which Section~\ref{sec:valid} counts as reuse) and 2019--2022 the check window, which this programme had read three times before the graph experiments (counted in Section~\ref{sec:valid}). The walk-forward zone was read once, for 2023, under a rule frozen in advance.
