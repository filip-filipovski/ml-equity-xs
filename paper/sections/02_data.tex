\section{Data and the point-in-time panel}\label{sec:data}

\paragraph{Universe and formation grid.}
Formation dates $t$ form one grid per horizon $h$: weekly (W) every fifth trading session from 2004-01-02, monthly (M) the last session of each month, quarterly (Q) the last session of March, June, September and December. The firm set $\mathcal U_t$ (``ALL'') contains every US common stock with a bar on $t$, a raw close of at least \$5 and a 63-session median dollar volume of at least \$1M; ``LIQ'' is the half of ALL whose median-dollar-volume percentile within the date exceeds 0.5; ``TOP500'' is the 500 largest by that volume. Prices are survivor-only: the feed lists firms as of October 2026, so delisted firms are absent (Section~\ref{sec:limits}). Let $r_{i,s}$ be the daily adjusted-close return (set missing if above $3$ or below $-0.95$) and $M_s$ the equal-weight mean return of the ALL universe fixed at the latest month-end strictly before $s$. The panels used by the main experiments run to 2022-12 and contain 1{,}664{,}463 weekly, 397{,}417 monthly and 132{,}961 quarterly firm-dates, of which 1{,}347{,}743 / 318{,}475 / 103{,}945 receive out-of-sample predictions.

\paragraph{Characteristics.}
Table~\ref{tab:features} lists the 28 characteristics as computed at formation row $t$. Fundamentals use SEC facts with acceptance time at or before the close of $t$ and go stale after 18 months; trailing twelve months (TTM) is the sum of four quarterly values or the latest annual. All eleven fundamental characteristics are set missing before 2009-01-01 and on rows whose implied daily turnover exceeds 10\%, so 2004--2008 rows carry only their missing-value flags. Earnings-event quantities use 8-K item 2.02 filings with a 16:00~ET session rule for day~0.

\paragraph{Rank transform.}
For each date and characteristic, finite values are winsorised at the 1st and 99th percentiles of that date, ranked with ties averaged ($R_i\in\{1,\dots,n\}$) and mapped to
\begin{equation}\label{eq:rank}
\tilde x_{i,f,t}=\frac{R_i-1}{n-1}-\frac12\in[-\tfrac12,\tfrac12],\qquad m_{i,f,t}=0;\qquad\text{missing: }\tilde x=0,\ m=1 .
\end{equation}
The model input is $x_{i,t}=(\tilde x_{i,1..28},\ \text{11 SIC one-hots},\ m_{i,1..28})\in\R^{67}$. The one-hots are the ten SIC divisions plus ``unknown'' from the firm's current filer profile and are static in time; a 29th candidate (gross profitability) was dropped by a 30\% coverage rule fixed before any result was read.

\paragraph{Target.}
With $u$ the next formation row, entry is the close of row $t{+}1$ and exit the close of row $u{+}1$:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:yraw}
y^{raw}_{i,t}=\prod_{s=t+2}^{u+1}(1+r_{i,s})-1,\qquad
y^{rank}_{i,t}=\frac{R(y^{raw}_{i,t})-1}{n_t-1}-\frac12,\qquad
y^{ex}_{i,t}=y^{raw}_{i,t}-\overline{y^{raw}_{t}},
\end{equation}
ranks over ALL with ties averaged. Models are trained on $y^{rank}$, information coefficients are scored against $y^{ex}$, and books are paid in $y^{raw}$. A within-date rank is not market-neutral: writing $r_{i,t}=a_t+(\beta_i-\bar\beta)\,\mathrm{mkt}_t+\varepsilon_{i,t}$, the rank keeps the term $(\beta_i-\bar\beta)\,\mathrm{mkt}_t$; measured, the monthly rank target has correlation 0.75 with the market excess return. Section~\ref{sec:books} removes this exposure at the portfolio stage.

\paragraph{Zones.}
The data are partitioned once and for all into a training zone (2004-01 to 2023-01), a walk-forward zone (2023-01 to 2024-07) and a sealed zone from 2024-07-01 from which no price or return has been read. Inside the training zone, 2009--2018 is the development window on which every selection was made (some later design choices were proposed after earlier check-window results had been reported, which Section~\ref{sec:valid} counts as reuse) and 2019--2022 the check window, which this programme had read three times before the graph experiments (disclosed and counted in Section~\ref{sec:valid}). The walk-forward zone was read once, for 2023, under a rule frozen in advance.
